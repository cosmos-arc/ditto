# Markets Screener contract feedback

- Retained the catalog toolbar, result surface, and comparison rail.
- Search and filters operate only on public identity fields: name, ticker, asset class, exchange, active status, and internal ID.
- Identity comparison is inline and functional. Price, valuation, market cap, industry, saved presets, exports, and generated pools were removed because their contracts are not public.
- The live search toolbar is intentionally two rows taller than the frozen compact filter bar, and the result list moves upward because the prototype-only valuation filters were removed; the recorded offsets preserve usable search space without fake controls.
